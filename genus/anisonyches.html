Genus

Anisonyches

Anisonyches is a genus of small, unplated marine heterotardigrades in the family Anisonychidae .

General Characteristics

Members of Anisonyches share a distinctive combination of cephalic appendages, leg structures, and claws. Adults typically have:

  • Four claws on legs I–III and three claws on leg IV
  • Claws inserted directly onto the legs
  • Paired basal spurs on the claws
  • A median cephalic cirrus
  • Internal, external, and lateral cephalic cirri, including cirrus E
  • Primary clavae, although these are absent in some species
  • Papillae on leg IV, which vary among species
  • Sensory organs on legs I–III, which vary among species
